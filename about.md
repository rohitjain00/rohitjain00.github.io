I am a Software Development Engineer with almost 5 years of experience building, scaling, and operating backend, web, and mobile systems. I have worked across Amazon Pay core services, Prime Video international expansion, developer tooling, automation, and early-stage crypto/web applications.

    A web application for adding recipes and searching for recipes online, built with Flask and MongoDB for the REST API and Angular for the frontend.
